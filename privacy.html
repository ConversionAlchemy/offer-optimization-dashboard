Privacy Policy

Offer Optimization Dashboard by Conversion Alchemy. Last updated 16 October 2026.

What the dashboard does with your numbers

The Offer Optimization Dashboard runs entirely in your web browser. The store numbers you enter are kept in your browser's own storage on your device. They are not sent to Conversion Alchemy, and we don't run a server that receives them.

If you connect Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you choose to, you sign in with Google and the dashboard saves your numbers as a file in your own Google Drive.

  • The dashboard asks for Google's drive.file permission only. This lets it see and change the files it created for you, and nothing else in your Drive.
  • Your files go straight from your browser to Google. Conversion Alchemy never sees them.
  • We don't store your Google account details or sign-in token. The token stays in your browser for the session and expires on its own.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the dashboard, or remove access in your Google Account settings. Your files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

The dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the page loads

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Both may record standard technical details such as your IP address when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.
